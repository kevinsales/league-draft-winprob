What moved the needle

ChampionWhereEffect 

How to read this. Effects are in percentage points of win chance. A positive number means the blue team wins slightly more often when that happens. Notice that even the strongest champion here is worth well under two games in a hundred — and that the total almost never leaves the 40–60% band. That is the finding: the draft barely decides a solo-queue game. What you do after champion select matters far more.

This is the champion-level model (logistic regression on picks and bans), used here so each pick's effect can be shown. A team-archetype model scores a hair higher — see the findings report. Not endorsed by Riot Games.
